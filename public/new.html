Sequence · 03

How a pour
actually goes.

Day 0

Layout & excavation

Strings up, sod and old surface out, dig to depth. You'll see the shape of it by lunch.

Day 0

Base & compaction

Granular A in lifts, plate-compacted, graded for fall away from the building.

Day 1

Forms & steel

Forms set and checked for level and slope. Mesh or fibre depending on what's driving over it.

Day 2

Pour & finish

Placed, screeded, floated, broomed. Timing is everything — finish too early and you seal water into the surface.

Day 2

Joints cut

Saw-cut within 12 hours. Concrete cracks; joints decide where it does.

Day 7

Drive on it

Walk at 24–48 hours, light vehicle at 7 days, full strength at 28.
